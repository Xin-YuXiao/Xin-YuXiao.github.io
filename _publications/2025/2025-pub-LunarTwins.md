abstract: >-
  Lunar Twins introduces domain-specific large language models and data for lunar exploration. The system includes the Chang'e and Yutu models, together with a collaborative multi-agent workflow for generating and solving lunar tasks. It also establishes a specialized lunar dataset integrating information from Chang'e missions. Experiments show that the resulting models outperform comparable general-purpose models on lunar-domain tasks.
